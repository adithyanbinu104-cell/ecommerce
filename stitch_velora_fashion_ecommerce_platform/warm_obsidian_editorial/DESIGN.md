## Brand & Style
The design system positions digital commerce at the intersection of haute couture editorial poise and high-velocity merchandising utility. The target audience seeks refined curation, effortless exploration, and architectural sophistication—reminiscent of bespoke atelier platforms and discerning fashion publications. 

The aesthetic style is **Warm Editorial Modernism**: high-contrast, razor-sharp typographic hierarchy juxtaposed with delicate hairline boundaries, serene warm ivory canvases, and unhurried whitespace. Tactility comes through micro-delicate borders and disciplined geometry rather than artificial drop shadows or skeumorphic textures. The interaction model remains assertive, whisper-quiet, and frictionless, ensuring fashion imagery commands primary visual authority while functional controls offer immediate tactile clarity.

## Colors
The color architecture rejects cold clinical grays in favor of warm, museum-grade parchment tones contrasted against deep obsidian pigment.

- **Primary (`#121212`)**: True deep obsidian black; commands primary calls-to-action, structural typography, brand headers, and high-impact micro-surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#C5A880`)**: Muted champagne gold; applied sparingly for exclusive tier indicators, refinement toggles, selective badges, and curated focal accents.
- **Tertiary (`#5A5854`)**: Slate charcoal; used for secondary editorial descriptions, specifications, meta properties, and inactive control states.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FDFBF7`)**: Warm ivory foundation, creating a tactile, linen-like background environment. Alternate nested canvas containers use soft alabaster (`#F7F5F0`).
- **Hairline Borders (`#EAE5DE`)**: Delicate whisper lines providing structural boundaries without disrupting the fluid editorial continuity.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm relies on an editorial dialogue between the high-contrast transitional serifs of Playfair Display and the clean geometric precision of Plus Jakarta Sans.

- Display and headline levels celebrate fashion editorial heritage, prioritizing optical breathing room and negative space over tight compaction.
- Body copy balances generous line heights with neutral sans-serif clarity to facilitate effortless browsing of product specifications, fabrication details, and care guides.
- Label, metadata, and badge tokens feature uppercase transformations paired with deliberate letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`), creating an understated luxury finish.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout architecture leverages a 12-column architectural grid on desktop screens (scaling down to a 6-column grid on tablets and a 4-column framework on mobile devices). 

- **E-commerce Hierarchy**: Product galleries deploy generous vertical rhythm to emphasize high-resolution garment detail, with product tiles running along strict proportional ratios (typically 3:4 or 4:5 vertical aspects).
- **Whitespace Cadence**: Outer page canvas margins remain spacious on desktop (`margin: 3rem`) to frame garments like gallery pieces, condensing gracefully to `margin-sm: 1.25rem` on mobile viewports for optimal tap targets and image-first feed immersion.
- **Section Rhythm**: Sections are separated by intentional negative space rather than heavy structural dividers, allowing garment imagery to dictate momentum.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is established through crisp surface layering, whisper-weight low-contrast outlines, and tonal transitions rather than volumetric drop shadows.

- **Surface Tiers**: Base views sit upon `#FDFBF7`. Overlaid cards, drawers, and flyouts shift slightly to crisp `#FFFFFF` or structured `#F7F5F0`.
- **Ghost Outlines**: Separation is mediated through 1px hairline rules tinted to `#EAE5DE`. Borders maintain consistent presence across cards, headers, and section splits.
- **Floating Modals & Sheets**: Quick-view dialogs, search takeovers, and mobile filter drawers utilize a subtle, wide-diffusion ambient blur (`rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.04) 0px 16px 48px`) behind a crisp 1px hairline border, preserving a pristine flat-art architectural finish.
- **Glass Elements**: Floating navigation bars and sticky checkout summaries apply a subtle frosted ivory backdrop blur (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) ensuring smooth visual transitions as editorial photography slides underneath.

## Shapes
The structural language leans into sharp architectural lines with soft micro-radii to maintain an uncompromising luxury posture.

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem`)**: Applied to interactive inputs, cards, image containers, and utility surfaces to remove digital harshness while retaining high-tailoring rigor.
- **Deliberate Geometry Exceptions**:
  - Primary product cards and editorial hero lookbooks favor razor-sharp rectilinear edges (`rounded-none`).
  - Badges, status tags, and size selector pills leverage full pill encapsulation (`rounded-full`) to contrast against rectangular garment photography.

## Components

- **Buttons**:
  - *Primary Action*: Solid `#121212` fill, `#FFFFFF` uppercase tracked typography, sharp rectilinear profile (`rounded-none` or `rounded-xs`), with a subtle opacity shift on hover.
  - *Secondary / Ghost*: Transparent surface bounded by a 1px `#121212` border; inverts to solid obsidian upon interaction.
  - *Luxury Text Button*: Pure text with a hairline animated underline that expands from left to right on interaction.

- **Badges & Chips**:
  - *Editorial Badges*: Jet black pill badges with crisp white 10px tracked typography for promotions ("NEW SEASON", "LIMITED EDITION").
  - *Atelier Accents*: Warm beige backgrounds (`#F4EFEB`) bounded by champagne borders (`#C5A880`) for tier and material provenance ("100% CASHMERE", "ARCHIVE").
  - *Filter Chips*: Neutral alabaster pills with 1px border `#EAE5DE`; solid `#121212` with white text when selected.

- **Inputs & Form Controls**:
  - *Field Style*: Understated bottom-border only (`1px solid #DFD9D0`) or full subtle alabaster container (`#F9F6F0`) with ghost border. Focus state elevates cleanly to a 1px `#121212` border with zero glow or chromatic ring.
  - *Checkboxes & Radios*: Geometric and minimal. Checkboxes feature crisp 1px squared obsidian boundaries; radios use concentric obsidian rings with zero soft gradients.

- **Cards**:
  - *Product Listing Cards*: Minimalist 3:4 portrait aspect ratio containers with seamless image display. No outer drop shadow. Metadata rests cleanly underneath the photo canvas: headline serif typography for the product title, slate charcoal for category tags, and jet black for price details.
  - *Editorial Collection Cards*: Spanned canvas tiles featuring high-contrast serif overlays, champagne metadata accents, and minimal border frames.

- **Luxury E-Commerce Additions**:
  - *Size Selector Carousel*: Squared and pill-shaped segmented controls featuring strikethroughs for out-of-stock sizes and instant obsidian fill upon selection.
  - *Sticky Commerce Bar*: Ambient ivory glass strip anchoring "Add to Bag", sizing guides, and wishlist buttons to the viewport floor during editorial scroll.
  - *Accordion Drawers*: Hairline-separated editorial accordions for "Composition & Care", "Fit & Silhouette", and "Complimentary Delivery", with whisper-thin plus/minus typographic toggles.
